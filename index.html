01 / ABOUT

About the
Department

The Commerce Department combines academic learning with practical exposure to business and professional life.

Students learn through classroom teaching, presentations, research, student-led societies, competitions and workshops. The department encourages curiosity, collaboration and a broad understanding of the changing business world.
